# Themes

## 1. Earthy & Organic Digital Gardens
These themes use natural, warm, and flora-inspired tones (greens, ochres, terracottas, and taupes). They rely on soft, muted backgrounds rather than pure black or white, minimizing glare and creating a cozy, organic feel.

|Name|Theme|Dominant Hues|Eyestrain Profile|Ideal Use Case|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|*botanical*|**[Botanical Moss & Terracotta](botanical.py)**|Sage green, deep slate, terracotta coral|Very Low (soft lichen & fog background)|Personal digital gardens, plant/biology wikis, creative logs|
|*sage*|**[Evergreen Sage & Soft Moss](sage.py)**|Deep spruce, soft sage, sea green|Very Low (deep spruce & light green fog)|Sustainability notes, long-form reading, field notebooks|
|*emerald*|**[Emerald Mindscape & Warm Amber](emerald.py)**|Obsidian teal, mint mist, warm amber|Low (rich dark forest & mint cream)|Deep research archives, executive knowledge bases|
|*sandstone*|**[Sandstone & Warm Ochre](sandstone.py)**|Warm taupe, oat milk, amber ochre|Minimal (tactile paper & stone neutrals)|Daily journaling, cozy essays, non-technical wikis|
|*gruvbox*|**[Gruvbox Analogue & Warm Rust](gruvbox.py)**|Dark charcoal, yellow paper, burnt orange|Low (retro high-warmth paper look)|Developer notes, CLI-inspired blogs, retro documentation|
|*orange*|**[Burnt Orange & Warm Charcoal](orange.py)**|Terracotta orange, warm amber, espresso charcoal, and soft oat linen|Very Low|Personal digital gardens, daily journaling, warm blog posts, non-technical wikis, and long-form reading|
|*lime*|**[Citrus Lime & Forest Slate](lime.py)**|Citrus lime green, leaf green, deep forest obsidian, and pale mint-fog|Low (sharp visual cues without causing optical fatigue)|Plant/nature digital gardens, modern developer notebooks, software component documentation, and high-energy personal wikis|

## 2. Cool Slate, Ocean & Technical Vaults
Characterized by blues, cyans, and slate greys, these themes offer structured, high-focus environments. They maintain high text contrast while using cool undertones to prevent fatigue during extended coding or writing sessions

|Name|Theme|Dominant Hues|Eyestrain Profile|Ideal Use Case|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|*dusk*|**[Twilight Dusk & Steel Slate](dusk.py)**|Midnight navy, breeze ice, steel blue|Very Low (calm, desaturated dusk blue)|Technical documentation, structured wikis, daily note-taking|
|*ocean*|**[Tranquil Ocean Fog & Mint](ocean.py)**|Oceanic midnight, ocean fog, seafoam teal|Very Low (cool mist & balanced mid-tones)|Academic research, serene reading environments|
|*nordic*|**[Nordic Frost & Electric Blue](nordic.py)**|Midnight slate, alabaster, cobalt blue|Moderate (higher contrast for crisp clarity)|Engineering notes, API docs, software architecture logs|
|*tokyo*|**[Tokyo Night & Cyber Cyan](tokyo.py)**|Dark indigo, metallic steel, neon lavender/cyan|Low–Moderate (IDE-inspired dark contrast)|Code-heavy vaults, developer portfolios, tech cheatsheets|

## 3. Soft Pastel & Low-Glare Atmospheric
These palettes prioritize extreme visual comfort by replacing stark contrasts with desaturated pastels, soft lavenders, and muted velvet backgrounds inspired by modern low-contrast themes (like Catppuccin and Rosé Pine).

|Name|Theme|Dominant Hues|Eyestrain Profile|Ideal Use Case|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|*peach*|**[Soft Pastel Slate & Peach](peach.py)**|Velvet night, oat silk, pastel peach|Minimal (ultra-soft, low contrast)|Late-night reading, general personal notes, reflective blogs|
|*mulberry*|**[Wisteria Bloom & Mulberry](muberry.py)**|Dark violet velvet, lilac silk, soft lavender|Very Low (rich dark velvet & soft tint)|Creative writing, philosophy, atmospheric personal wikis|

## 4. Literary, Editorial & Classic Foundations
These themes draw inspiration from print media, typography, and vintage reference materials. They feature high text readability, warm parchment backdrops, and serif or high-legibility fonts.

|Name|Theme|Dominant Hues|Eyestrain Profile|Ideal Use Case|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|*burgundy*|**[Literary Parchment & Burgundy](burgundy.py)**|Roasted espresso, parchment white, crimson|Low (soft warm parchment base)|Essays, long-form literature, history, book reviews|
|*solarized*|**[Authentic Solarized](solarized.py)**|Base03 deep teal, Base3 warm cream, Solarized blue|Low (precision-calibrated color spaces)|Classic developer wikis, multi-purpose documentation|

# Quick Selection Matrix
- **For Maximum Eyestrain Relief (Late Night / Extended Hours)**: [Soft Pastel Slate & Peach](peach.py), [Tranquil Ocean Fog & Mint](ocean.py), or [Sandstone & Warm Ochre](sandstone.py).
- **For Technical / Software Vaults**: [Twilight Dusk & Steel Slate](dusk.py), [Tokyo Night](tokyo.py), or [Nordic Frost](nordic.py).
- **For Organic / Personal Knowledge Gardens**: [Botanical Moss & Terracotta](botanical.py), [Evergreen Sage & Soft Moss](sage.py), [Burnt Orange & Warm Charcoal](orange.py), or [Citrus Lime & Forest Slate](lime.py).
- **For Long-Form Writing & Essays**: [Literary Parchment & Burgundy](burgundy.py). 
- **For Calibrated Classic Precision**: [Authentic Solarized](solarized.py).
